Tommo's priority scale: **Urgent = "drop all and do it"** — reserved for live emergencies, never for ordinary important work. Work inside a project that isn't planned out yet caps at High, and "important" items within it are usually Medium.

**Why:** I filed a sky-capture task as Urgent because he called it "most important" among peers; he had me rescale the whole batch down one step (Urgent→High, High→Medium).

**How to apply:** when filing batches, express relative importance one notch lower than instinct: the standout item gets High, the rest Medium, nice-to-haves Low/Backlog. Urgent only when something is actively broken for players or blocking the team today.
